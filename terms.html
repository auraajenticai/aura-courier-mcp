1. Scope of Service

Aura Courier MCP (https://courier.auraajenticai.cloud) provides a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server and logistics integration gateway enabling AI assistants (including Google Gemini, Antigravity, Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and custom agents) and e-commerce stores to interact with supported Bangladeshi courier providers (Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, Paperfly) and Google Maps Platform spatial APIs.

2. Authentication, OAuth 2.1 & Credential Responsibility

Access to protected endpoints is governed via OAuth 2.1 with PKCE (S256), Aura Cloud SSO, scoped Merchant API keys, or Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) request headers.

  • You are solely responsible for safeguarding your Aura account credentials, API keys, and third-party carrier credentials.
  • OAuth tokens may be revoked at any time via POST /oauth/revoke or through your Aura Cloud Console.
  • Read-Only Sandbox access is strictly limited to non-mutating discovery and estimation tools.

3. Carrier Dispatch, Fees & Billable Actions

State-mutating tools such as create_parcel, bulk_create_parcels, update_parcel, and cancel_parcel transmit real operational requests directly to third-party courier networks when invoked with write credentials (unless dry_run: true is specified).

  • All courier shipping charges, COD settlement timelines, pickup schedules, and parcel insurance terms are governed directly by your agreement with Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, or Paperfly.
  • We recommend using dry_run: true or human-in-the-loop confirmation before dispatching high-value COD shipments.

4. Acceptable Use & Rate Limits

  • Do not abuse fraud-check, geocoding, or carrier APIs for unauthorized scraping, harassment, or unlawful surveillance.
  • Fair-use rate limits (per-key and per-IP) apply to protect carrier upstream quotas and platform availability.
  • Any automated or human-initiated misuse may result in immediate key suspension or revocation.

5. Privacy, Retention & Open-Source License

Personal data handling, PII masking on public tracking pages, 90-day automated anonymization, and zero-knowledge credential isolation are detailed in our Privacy Policy. The Aura Courier MCP open-source codebase is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

6. Disclaimer & Support

Aura Courier MCP is provided "as is" without warranties regarding third-party courier API uptime or carrier delivery SLAs. For enterprise support, compliance inquiries, or security disclosures, contact ceo@auraajenticai.cloud.
